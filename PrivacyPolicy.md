# Privacy Policy — Bitcoin Price Tracker for Microsoft Edge

**Last updated:** October 14, 2026

Bitcoin Price Tracker displays the Bitcoin price in the Edge toolbar and stores the display preferences you choose.

## Information the extension handles

- **Preferences:** Your selected currency (USD or EUR) and refresh interval (1, 5, 10, or 30 minutes) are stored using Microsoft Edge's `storage.sync` extension API. Edge may synchronize these settings through your signed-in browser profile. The extension does not receive or store your Microsoft account credentials.
- **Price requests:** The extension requests the public Bitcoin price from CoinGecko over HTTPS. The request contains the fixed currency pair (USD and EUR); it does not include your browsing history, page contents, account credentials, or other personal information. CoinGecko may receive standard connection metadata such as your IP address under its own privacy policy.
- **Diagnostics:** The extension does not include analytics, advertising, tracking, or remote executable code. Errors are only written to the browser extension's local developer console.

## How to control or remove information

Change currency or refresh interval from the extension popup. You can remove the extension or clear its stored data in Edge to delete its saved preferences. The extension does not send preferences to the developer.

## Third-party service

Bitcoin prices are provided by [CoinGecko](https://www.coingecko.com/en/api). Requests are made only to `https://api.coingecko.com/`. CoinGecko's handling of connection data is governed by its own policies.

## Changes and contact

This policy will be updated if the extension's data practices change. For questions or privacy requests, contact the publisher through the support contact shown on the Microsoft Edge Add-ons listing.
